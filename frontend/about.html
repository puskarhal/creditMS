Eduplex Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

Where excellence is effortless…

Incepted in the year 2013 by a group of professionals with enriched MNC experience, Eduplex Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (ESPL) began its journey to emerge as an outstanding institution rather than just a business entity. Starting in the education space, it gradually expanded into Managed Services, Consulting, and IT Projects — particularly digitization — keeping pace with the dynamic changes in the business landscape.

ESPL is part of the Start-up India and UDYAM initiatives, also supported by the National Skill Management framework. It maintains a firm PAN India presence, operating from 23 different locations with 120+ experienced individuals and associated partners.

The organization operates across two domains — Managed Services and Projects. Under Managed Services, it offers HR & Payroll processing, skill supplementation, start-up/pilot consulting, and operations management, along with Field and Resident Engineer support and training for engineering and general students. Under Projects, ESPL provides end-to-end digitization solutions for its customer base.
